Our Story

Dedicated to Solving Your Plumbing Nightmares

Shri Plumbing Solutions was founded with a simple mission: to deliver honest, reliable, and high-quality plumbing services to the residents and businesses of Bhosari, PCMC, and Pune.

Whether it's a midnight emergency or a planned luxury bathroom installation, we treat every job with the same level of professionalism and urgency.

Today, backed by a team of licensed, insured, and background-checked technicians, we continue to raise the bar for what you should expect from a plumbing company.
